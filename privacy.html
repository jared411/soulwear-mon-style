Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. What we collect

When you place an order or make an enquiry, we collect your name, phone number, email address (if you give it), delivery address and any notes you add. We also keep records of your orders. Our website does not require you to create an account.

2. Why we collect it

We use your information to take and deliver your order, reply to your questions, arrange payment and delivery, and keep records that the law requires. We do not sell your information.

3. Who we share it with

We share your details only with people who help us run the business, such as delivery riders and our messaging and hosting providers, and only as far as needed. We may also share them if the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting and warranty purposes, and delete other information when it is no longer needed.

5. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. To do this, contact us on WhatsApp or at breemohbeautyglow@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website is completely secure, so please share only what is needed for your order.

7. Cookies

This website saves your shopping cart on your own device so it stays there when you come back. We do not use advertising cookies.
