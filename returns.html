Returns and Exchanges

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Your rights first

Nothing on this page takes away your rights under Kenya's Consumer Protection Act, 2012. If an item is faulty, damaged or not as described, we will repair, replace or refund it.

Exchanges

You can exchange an item for a different size or colour within 7 days of delivery, if it is unworn, unwashed, with its tags still attached and in its original condition. Tell us on WhatsApp first, and we will arrange the exchange with you.

Refunds

We refund orders that were cancelled by us, or items that are faulty or not as described, in full. Refunds are paid back to the method you used to pay, within 7 working days after we receive the item or confirm the cancellation.

Items we cannot take back

For hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns of worn or washed items, or items without their tags. Sale items are final unless faulty.

Who pays for returns

If the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item, we cover the return delivery. If you change your mind, you pay the return delivery cost.

How to start a return or exchange

Send us a message on WhatsApp at +254 707 073 011 or +254 797 770 150, with your order details and photos of the item. We reply within one working day.
